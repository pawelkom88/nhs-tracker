# Launch Readiness Checklist and Sign-off — Know Your Wait

This document serves as the canonical pre-launch gate and verification record for **Know Your Wait** ([ADR-0004](./adr/0004-name-without-nhs.md)). It consolidates automated pipeline and build criteria, editorial reviews, accessibility audits, identity guidelines, and deployment readiness checks before public release.

Every item must either be marked **`[x] VERIFIED`** with reproducible evidence or **`[ ] DEFERRED`** with explicit Product Owner sign-off and conditions for production switchover.

### Content Policy Verifications

- `[x] VERIFIED`: **No Banned / Avoid Terms.** Text scanned to ensure terms like "average", "expected wait", "estimated wait", "breach", "52-week wait", "private hospital", "near you", or "local area" do not appear in user-facing content (enforced by `test/guards/avoid-terms.test.ts` and `scripts/scan-output.ts`).
- `[x] VERIFIED`: **Snapshots, Not Predictions.** Every figure and explainer page carries the fixed explanatory caption clarifying that waiting times are retrospective snapshots and not a guarantee or prediction of an individual patient's wait.
- `[x] VERIFIED`: **Border Residence Guidance.** Prominent "Live near the border?" notice on all nation and specialty pages reminding users that NHS entitlements follow GP registration ([ADR-0001](./adr/0001-home-nation-is-residence.md)).
- `[x] VERIFIED`: **Not Medical Advice Disclaimer.** Every page includes prominent header/footer notices stating that Know Your Wait does not provide medical advice and directing users with urgent symptoms to NHS 111 or their GP.

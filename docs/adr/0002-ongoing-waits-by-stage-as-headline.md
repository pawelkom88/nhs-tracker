# Headline figures are Ongoing Waits, split by Wait Stage, never summed

England and Wales publish ongoing waits for the whole referral-to-treatment pathway; Scotland publishes ongoing waits only per stage (new outpatient, inpatient/day case), and its RTT figure covers completed pathways only. We headline Ongoing Waits everywhere, tag every measure with its Wait Stage, and show Scotland's stages as separate figures. We never add Scotland's stages to approximate a total pathway wait, because different patients are counted at each stage and the gaps between stages aren't measured.

## Consequences

- Completed Waits are excluded from v1 headlines because patients misread them as "how long it takes".
- Every headline is presented as a snapshot of people currently waiting at a reporting date, never as a prediction for the user.

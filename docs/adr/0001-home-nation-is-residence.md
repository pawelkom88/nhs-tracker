# Home Nation is nation of residence, not GP registration

NHS entitlement and patient choice rights follow the nation of the user's GP practice, not where they live; border residents (e.g. Chepstow) can differ. We still ask "which nation do you live in?" because average users understand residence, and asking about GP registration or showing a separate cross-border entitlement view adds explanation burden that confuses more users than it helps. The GP-registration nuance is covered in explanatory text only.

## Consequences

- Cross-border support in v1 is explanatory only: a Home Nation's pages never show another Publishing Nation's statistics. A visible "Live near the border?" notice tells users to check each nation's pages separately and talk to their GP.
- This narrows the PRD's Chepstow example: that user switches Home Nation to see English data; the product does not put the two side by side.

# Product is named "Know Your Wait", without "NHS"

The draft name "NHS Wait Finder" used the NHS name, which is protected and whose identity guidance discourages non-NHS organisations from using it in ways that imply affiliation; it would also contradict our independence disclaimer and invite users to assume the site is official. We chose "Know Your Wait": no NHS in name, logo or domain, with "NHS" used only descriptively in taglines and copy. "Know" over "Find" because the product explains figures rather than helping users pick a provider.

# Include Independent Sector Providers as Reporting Units

England's official RTT data includes independent sector providers that treat NHS-funded patients, a large share of planned orthopaedic care, and these are legitimate free choices at referral. Although the PRD excludes private healthcare, we include them as ordinary Reporting Units, labelled as NHS-funded and free to the patient, because the exclusion targets selling private care, and leaving them out would hide real NHS options and make trust-only figures look complete.

## Consequences

- Presentation must stay neutral to avoid looking promotional: no ranking, no shortest-wait sort, no outbound links to provider websites.
- Scotland's Golden Jubilee (NHS National Waiting Times Centre) is likewise shown as a normal health board, noted as treating patients referred from all boards.

# Know Your Wait

Patient-facing explanations of official NHS waiting-time statistics for England, Wales and Scotland. Each nation's data and terminology stay separate.

## Language

**Home Nation**:
The nation (England, Wales or Scotland) the user says they live in. Decides which NHS process and patient-options explanations are shown.
_Avoid_: Country, user nation, region

**Publishing Nation**:
The nation whose NHS body published a given waiting-time statistic. Decides the definitions and labels that apply to that statistic.
_Avoid_: Data source country, provider nation

**Specialty**:
A category of planned care exactly as a Publishing Nation defines it, under that nation's official name. Specialties from different nations are never treated as equivalent, even when names match.
_Avoid_: Service, department, category. "Treatment function" is the official name in England and Wales; use it only when quoting their data.

**Ongoing Wait**:
How long people currently on a waiting list have waited so far, as of a Publishing Nation's reporting date. A snapshot of the list, never a forecast of any individual's wait.
_Avoid_: Expected wait, estimated wait, wait time (unqualified)

**Completed Wait**:
How long people who have already been treated (or reached the end of a stage) waited. Not used for headline figures.
_Avoid_: Actual wait, wait time (unqualified)

**Wait Stage**:
The part of the patient journey a wait measure covers, as defined by the Publishing Nation: whole referral-to-treatment pathway, first outpatient appointment, or inpatient/day case treatment. Measures for different Wait Stages are never added together.
_Avoid_: Step, phase, pathway (unqualified)

**Statistic**:
What a figure measures, as defined by its Publishing Nation, e.g. Median Ongoing Wait, percentage within the Waiting Standard, Long Wait count. Statistics from different nations are never treated as the same Statistic.
_Avoid_: Metric, KPI, measure (unqualified)

**Median Ongoing Wait**:
The Ongoing Wait that half the people on a list have waited up to, as officially published by the Publishing Nation. Used for headline figures.
_Avoid_: Average wait, mean wait, typical wait (unless "half of people" is spelled out)

**Waiting Standard**:
A Publishing Nation's official target for a Wait Stage, e.g. 18 weeks (England RTT), 26 weeks (Wales RTT), 12 weeks (Scotland, per stage). Only meaningful within that nation.
_Avoid_: Target (unqualified), limit, guarantee

**Reporting Unit**:
The organisation a Publishing Nation reports wait figures against: an NHS trust or Independent Sector Provider in England, a health board in Wales and Scotland. Shown to users under the nation's own word for it.
_Avoid_: Provider, hospital, organisation (unqualified)

**Hospital Site**:
A physical hospital run by a Reporting Unit. Context only; no wait figures are published for it.
_Avoid_: Hospital (when Reporting Unit is meant), location

**Reporting Period**:
The point in time a published wait figure describes, e.g. the list as at 31 August 2026. Headline wording always uses this date.
_Avoid_: Date (unqualified), month, data date

**Publication Date**:
When the Publishing Nation released a figure. Always later than the Reporting Period, often by six weeks or more.
_Avoid_: Last updated, date (unqualified)

**Year-Earlier Comparison**:
The same measure for the same Reporting Unit and Specialty, twelve months before the Reporting Period. Omitted when the Reporting Unit or Specialty is not the same entity in both periods.
_Avoid_: Trend, change, history

**Official Figure**:
A statistic republished unchanged from a Publishing Nation's release, always shown with its source, Reporting Period and Publication Date.
_Avoid_: Data (unqualified), our figures

**Derived Figure**:
A figure Know Your Wait calculates by exactly adding Official Figures from the same release, e.g. summing published wait bands. Labelled as calculated and shown with its sources. Estimates are never Derived Figures.
_Avoid_: Estimate, calculated wait, our data

**Editorial Content**:
Explanatory text written by Know Your Wait (specialty descriptions, process guides, caveats). Never contains figures other than Official or Derived Figures.
_Avoid_: Guidance, advice

**NHS Region**:
One of NHS England's seven regions, used only to group English Reporting Units for browsing. Not a Home Nation and not a comparison unit.
_Avoid_: Area, area near you, local area

**Independent Sector Provider**:
A non-NHS organisation that treats NHS-funded patients, free to the patient, and appears in official waiting figures as a Reporting Unit.
_Avoid_: Private hospital, private provider

**Patient Right**:
An entitlement a Home Nation gives patients in law or in its NHS constitution, e.g. England's right to choose provider at referral, Scotland's Treatment Time Guarantee. Distinct from a Waiting Standard, which is a target, not an entitlement.
_Avoid_: Option, entitlement (unqualified), guarantee (unless legally one)

**Long Wait**:
An Ongoing Wait of over one year. Counted from an Official Figure, or a Derived Figure where the nation publishes only bands. Never estimated.
_Avoid_: Breach, very long wait, 52-week wait (in user-facing text)

### Figure status

**Suppressed Figure**:
A value the Publishing Nation withheld, typically to protect privacy with small numbers. Shown as not published; never as zero or blank.
_Avoid_: Missing, zero, n/a

**Provisional Figure**:
A value the Publishing Nation marks as subject to revision.
_Avoid_: Draft, estimate

**Carried-Forward Figure**:
The last available figures for a Reporting Unit absent from the latest release, shown with their own older Reporting Period and a warning, for up to 12 months.
_Avoid_: Latest, current, stale data

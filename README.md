# Know Your Wait

A free, independent site that explains official NHS waiting-time statistics for England, Wales and Scotland in plain English.

## About

NHS waiting-time statistics are public, but spread across separate NHS and government sites in each nation and presented for performance reporting, not for patients. Know Your Wait republishes Official Figures with clear explanations.

Know Your Wait is an independent information site. It is not part of, or endorsed by, the NHS, NHS England, NHS Wales, NHS Scotland or any government. It does not sell healthcare, insurance or referrals, and it does not tell users which provider to choose.
